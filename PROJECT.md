# Project: AERION / Aeterna Luxury Watches

## Vision
To create a premium, high-end landing page for a luxury watch brand that delivers a cinematic and immersive experience. The goal is to blend modern aesthetics (dark mode, glassmorphism, gold accents) with high-performance animations and 3D-like visual effects to "WOW" the user.

## Context
- **Current Stack**: HTML5, Vanilla CSS, Javascript.
- **Aesthetic**: Premium Dark Mode, Muted Gold, Sleek Typography.
- **Key Features**: Hero cinematic video, interactive scroll animations, storytelling sections.

## Constraints
- Must be responsive (Mobile & Desktop).
- High performance (zero scroll lag).
- No external heavy frameworks unless necessary.
